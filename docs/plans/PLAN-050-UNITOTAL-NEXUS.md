## Architecture Decision Record: ADR-063
**Title**: Synthesis of Gnostic 10-Sephiroth Integrity Vector and Universal Multi-Fleet Orchestration  
**Status**: APPROVED  
**Context**:  
As EOS reaches maturity across 62 specifications, managing multiple independent client and sovereign repositories requires a unified architectural doctrine that links fundamental philosophical intentionality (Samaelian Gnosis) to deterministic software engineering verification (Google DeepMind / AWS / Meta standards).

**Decision**:  
Implement `EosSamaelUnitotalNexus` as a Layer-0 TypeScript engine that:
1. Codifies the 10 Sephiroth as an objective 10-dimensional architectural health vector:
   - `KETHER`: Specification as Supreme Truth (EARS/BDD coverage).
   - `CHOKMAH`: Evolutionary optimization and mutation search (AST purity).
   - `BINAH`: Formal verification, TLA+ mathematical models, and TLC checks.
   - `CHESED`: Blameless governance, ADR documentation, and consensus quorums.
   - `GEBURAH`: FDIR self-healing, zero plain secrets, and SSRF shielding.
   - `TIPHERETH`: Acyclic subagent topology and zero-copy coordination.
   - `NETZACH`: Model routing efficiency (Flash-to-Pro cascade, token savings).
   - `HOD`: Monotonic SQLite WAL Ledger with cryptographic SHA-256 chaining.
   - `YESOD`: Low-level mechanical sympathy (zero allocations, LMAX ring buffers).
   - `MALKUTH`: Real production operational delivery and live probe verification.
2. Provides automated multi-fleet inspection across all 5 registered repositories.
3. Automatically evaluates and aligns developer harness configurations (.cursor/mcp.json, engram paths).
4. Persists cryptographic receipts in the durable SQLite WAL ledger.

**Rejected Alternatives**:
1. *Pure Esoteric Allegory Without Executable Tests*: Rejected per Law I and Law III.
2. *Fragmented Per-Repo Manual Maintenance*: Rejected per Law IV and Law V.
