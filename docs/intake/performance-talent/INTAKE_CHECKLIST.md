# Intake Checklist: Performance Talent Group

Use this checklist to ensure all essential inputs are collected from the client to guarantee immediate, same-day resolution.

---

## 1. Brand Identity Assets
- [ ] **Logo Files**: High-resolution Vector SVG, transparent PNG (light and dark mode variants).
- [ ] **Brand Guidelines / Colors**: Primary brand hex codes, accent color, secondary tones.
- [ ] **Typography Preferences**: Corporate font family (Inter, Outfit, Plus Jakarta Sans, etc.).
- [ ] **Favicon**: Square 512x512 PNG or SVG icon.

## 2. Institutional Copy & Content
- [ ] **Value Proposition Headline**: Main hero statement (e.g., "Connecting Exceptional Leadership with High-Growth Organizations").
- [ ] **Practice Areas / Services**: 3 to 6 key specializations (Executive Search, Technical Leadership, Interim Management, Advisory).
- [ ] **About Us / Story**: 2-3 paragraphs detailing the firm's philosophy, track record, and methodology.
- [ ] **Metrics & Proof Points**: Key statistics (e.g., "98% placement retention", "15+ years average search partner experience", "500+ leaders placed").
- [ ] **Testimonials / Client Quotes**: 2-4 endorsements from corporate clients or executive placements.

## 3. Contact, Conversion & Legal
- [ ] **Corporate Email & Phone**: Official inquiries email (e.g., `contact@performancetalent.com`), WhatsApp business number.
- [ ] **Office Location**: Headquarters city, physical address (or indication of global/remote operations).
- [ ] **Legal Details**: Official company legal name, registration/tax number for footer compliance.
- [ ] **Social Profiles**: LinkedIn corporate page URL.

## 4. Hosting & Domain Deployment
- [ ] **Target Domain Name**: (e.g., `performancetalentgroup.com`).
- [ ] **DNS Provider**: Cloudflare, GoDaddy, Namecheap, etc.
- [ ] **Hosting Target**: Vercel (Recommended) or Netlify.
